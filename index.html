02 — SEASONAL DISCOVERY

New seasonal records reveal two different patterns

Citizen-science observations are not only adding more butterfly records. They are also documenting some species during months outside their previously recorded seasonal windows.

Across all species, these new records concentrate in particular months of the year. Looking at individual butterflies, however, reveals that the pattern differs considerably between species.

How widespread are the seasonal additions?

Each dot represents one butterfly species in the seasonal-record file, grouped by its number of newly documented months. Dots wrap into rows of five; labels show the total species in each group. Hover over a dot to see the species and months.
